Features

Everything you need to get PDFs done

A complete toolkit for annotating, editing and organizing documents — designed to feel effortless.

Edit existing text

Click any line of text in your PDF to replace it. Folio matches the size and position automatically.

Add text anywhere

Fill forms, add notes and labels with full control over font, size, weight, style and color.

eSign documents

Draw, type or upload your signature. It's saved locally so you can sign the next doc in seconds.

Highlight & annotate

Highlight passages, draw freehand, add arrows, rectangles, circles and lines with precision.

Merge & organize

Watermarks

Privacy by design

Your documents never leave your device

Most online PDF editors upload your files to their servers. Folio runs entirely in your browser, so contracts, invoices and medical records stay yours.

  • Processing happens 100% locally with WebAssembly-speed JavaScript
  • Autosave stores your work only in your browser's private storage
  • No accounts, tracking pixels or file retention — ever

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Folio PDF really free?

Yes. Every editing tool is free to use with no watermarks and no limits on the number of documents.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Folio reads and writes PDFs entirely inside your browser. Your documents are never sent to a server.

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Choose the "Edit text" tool and click any line. Folio covers the original and places editable text with the same size and position.

Will my edits be visible in other PDF readers?

Absolutely. Edits are flattened into the PDF on export, so they look identical in Acrobat, Preview, Chrome and mobile readers.

What happens if I close the tab?

Your work is autosaved to your browser's local storage. Reopen the editor and you'll pick up right where you left off.
